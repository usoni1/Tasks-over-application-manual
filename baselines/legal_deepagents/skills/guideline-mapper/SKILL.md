name: guideline-mapper
description: Map a verified statute of conviction to the correct Chapter Two guideline using USSG Appendix A and structural fallback browsing.
---

Use Appendix A to map the statute of conviction to candidate Chapter Two guideline sections. Do not skip this step. If multiple sections are returned, inspect candidates and retain only those supported by the statute and case facts.

If Appendix A has no exact match, do not retry the same lookup. Use USSG structural tools: inspect the chapter table of contents, open the relevant subheading, then inspect the candidate section text or statutory-provisions block. Do not choose a guideline from memory or general legal knowledge.

Return the mapping evidence, candidate sections considered, selected controlling section or unresolved ambiguity, and the inspected text supporting the result.
